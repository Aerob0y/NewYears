Can a package or half-board bring the cost down?

Yes, but compare inclusions carefully. Half-board generally covers breakfast and dinner, not mountain lunch, drinks or ski passes. Tour-operator packages can save on bundled flights, baggage and transfers, while independent hotel + lift-pass packages may be better value for groups. Prices below are advertised from prices for the stated departure, not a six-person guaranteed quote.
